# Decision: The Memory Layer is standalone, founded on the training material

**TL;DR:** The second-brain training is its own playlist. It may still be broken into multiple parts. It may use all of the existing training material and documentation as founding knowledge, and its reference build covers the full retrieval stack.

**Decided:** 2026-10-21  ·  **By:** Mark  ·  **Status:** active

## Context
The first plan was "Deep Dive C" inside the [[train-the-trainer-series]]. The topic, though, is tool-agnostic and stands on its own: it is not limited to Obsidian.

## Decision
- The series is its own playlist ([[the-memory-layer]]). It can be split into parts.
- Founding knowledge is the [[ai-training-guide]], the Session 1–8 and Deep Dive A/B transcripts, and the two research briefs.
- The build covers the full stack: files plus index, hybrid search with local embeddings, an MCP server, and a heartbeat.
- Obsidian is the recommended front end. The vault also works as a plain folder in any harness.

## Why
A standalone playlist reaches people outside the train-the-trainer audience. Seeding the brain with real program material makes the demo useful from day one.

## Consequences / follow-ups
- Name the series and its parts.
- Build decks, narration and labs per part.
